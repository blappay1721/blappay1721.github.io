Persona chatbot and Discord bot platform

A fine-tuned 1.7B model that replies in each friend's own voice

330kmessages from 8 years of chat
  • Built the training set from 330,000 Discord messages: scrubbed personal info, merged alt accounts and split 8 years of history into 16,581 conversations.
  • Fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B with QLoRA on a single 12 GB GPU (64,000 examples, 36 personas), then a DPO pass that ships only if it beats the SFT model on held-out chats. Runs locally, quantized to 4-bit.
  • Retrieval from two indexes: the persona's own past lines for their voice, and past conversations for facts when relevant. A persona only “remembers” DMs they were in.
  • Evaluation showed factual recall was the weak point, so a rebuild around verified facts is in progress.
  • In Discord, /chat opens a channel where the persona posts under their own name and avatar, taking turns with up to 5 people. Needs no public URL or open ports.

Self-hosted game server platform
